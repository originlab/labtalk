3.5.3.2.14 Loglogisticpdf

loglogisticpdf-func

Description:

probability density function for loglogistic

Syntax:

double loglogisticpdf(const double x, double mu, double sigma)

Parameters:

x (input, double)
The value of the x variate.
mu (input, double)
Location parameter of the distribution
sigma (input, double)
The scale parameter, must be positive
