3.5.3.3.15 Logisticinv

logisticinv-func

Description:

INV for Logistic

Syntax:

double logisticinv(double dP, double mu, double sigma)

Parameters:

dP (input, double)
The target probability value
mu (input, double)
The location parameter representing the mean (median) of the distribution.
sigma (input, double)
The positive scale parameter proportional to the standard deviation.
